Executive Summary

This proposal outlines the premium-tier infrastructure, platform development, and operational budget to support **AYURDISHA** under extreme concurrent user volumes. To guarantee absolute uptime during peak hours of the 11th World Ayurveda Congress (with an estimated 5,000+ concurrent active delegates registering and interacting with the platform), this premium scenario utilizes Vercel Enterprise scaling, professional transactional OTP gateways, and Antigravity AI Ultra capabilities.

The standard free or lower paid tiers will **inevitably throttle or fail** during the live opening hours when thousands of students verify their emails simultaneously.

This premium architecture uses **SendGrid/Resend Pro transactional APIs** to ensure sub-second 6-digit OTP delivery, alongside **Vercel Enterprise** to handle infinite concurrent serverless queries and prevent DDoS/automated bot registrations.
